Your Spending Can Help You Save

Instead of having to remember to move money into savings, Spend & Save is doing it automatically. Choose how much you want to save from each eligible transaction, and that amount is moved into your dedicated savings wallet whenever you spend.

You can choose a savings rate between 1% and 15%, giving you control over how much you set aside.

Choose How You Want to Save

Not every goal needs the same approach. That's why Spend & Save gives you two ways to put money aside.

Flexible Savings

For goals where you want the freedom to save, earn interest on your savings, and access your money when you need it.

Your savings remain available to withdraw at any time, with no lock-in period or penalty for withdrawing. You can also turn the automatic saving feature off whenever you choose.

Best for

Building a habit, creating a financial cushion, or saving while keeping easy access to your money.

Locked Savings

For goals that need a little more commitment.

Set money aside for a defined period and earn interest on your locked savings, helping you stay focused on the goal while your money grows.

Best for

Longer-term goals, planned expenses, and money you're happy to leave untouched for a set period.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I save from each transaction? −

You can choose a savings rate between 1% and 15% of eligible transactions.

Which transactions qualify? +

Eligible transactions are transfers, bill payments and card spending

Can I access my Flexible Savings whenever I need it? +

Yes. Flexible Savings can be withdrawn at any time without a lock-in period or penalty.

Can I stop Spend & Save? +

Yes. You can deactivate the feature at any time, with the change taking effect from your next transaction.

How does Locked Savings work? +

Locked Savings allows you to save for a defined period and earn interest on the amount saved.

Can I withdraw from Locked Savings before the end of the lock period? +

Yes, but early withdrawal would lead to forfeiture of the interest accumulated.

What interest rate will I earn? +

Earn up to 2% per month. Interest is dependent on the type of product and amount. The actual rates will be displayed on activation of the service.
