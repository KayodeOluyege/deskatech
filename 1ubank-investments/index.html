What to Consider Before Investing

Every investment carries some level of risk, and different options are suited to different financial goals.

Before investing, be clear on the following:

What you're investing in

How long it will be tied up

The returns you expect

The risks involved

When you can access your money

Any fees that apply

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can invest? −

Everyone with investable funds.

What investment options are available? +

Flexible, Fixed Income, and Money Market Fund subscriptions.

What is the minimum amount I can invest? +

₦5,000.

How long do I need to keep my money invested? +

Between 1 day and 365 days.

How are returns paid? +

Returns are calculated monthly, so you can monitor your progress, and can be credited either upfront or on maturity.

Can I withdraw my money before maturity? +

Yes, for flexible income. For fixed income, there will be a forfeiture on returns, while some locked products cannot be accessed until maturity.

Are there fees or charges? +

A withholding tax of 10% applies.

How can I track my investments? +

Your interest earned can be viewed in-app as it progresses.
