Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for a personal loan? −

Salary earners and anyone with a verifiable source of income.

How do I apply? +

You can apply for a loan via any of our channels.

How much can I borrow? +

This will be determined by your earning capacity.

What interest rate will I pay? +

0.01% daily.

How long do I have to repay the loan? +

The repayment time is flexible; however, the maximum time is 24 months.

How is my eligibility determined? +

A credit assessment criterion will be carried out.

Can I repay my loan early? +

Yes, your loans can be repaid before the due date.

What happens if I miss a repayment? +

If you miss a repayment, a penalty on interest will be applied.
