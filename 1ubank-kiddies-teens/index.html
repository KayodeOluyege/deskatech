Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the Kiddies & Teens account for? −

Children between the ages of 11 and 17.

Who can open the account? +

Parents with a 1U account.

Can parents monitor the account? +

Yes. Parents have full access to their children's or wards' accounts.

Can parents set spending limits? +

Yes. Parents can set limits to monitor their children's spending habits.

Can the child or teen save money? +

Yes, they're able to save money.

Can money be sent into the account? +

Yes, funds can be transferred into the Kiddie account.

Can the child or teen make payments? +

Yes, the Kiddie account allows payments.

What happens when the child turns 18? +

Parents or guardians are notified three months before the child's 18th birthday, so they can upgrade the account and prepare to transfer ownership to their ward.

Give Them a Stronger Start

Good money habits don't begin when they become adults. They begin with the small choices they're making today.
